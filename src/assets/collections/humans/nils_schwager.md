Nils works as a Research Fellow at the Department of Computational Linguistics at Trier University. His thesis examines when LM-based instruments can be trusted to produce new scientific insight, arguing that accuracy and calibration are distinct properties which current applications rarely establish together. His research on accuracy and calibration of Human Simulacra grounds EVAS's case for stronger validation in the mechanics of the models themselves.
