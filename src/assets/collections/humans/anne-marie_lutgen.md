Anne-Marie is a doctoral student at the Department of Luxembourgish at the University of Luxembourg. She has a background in both linguistics and computational linguistics and her research takes an interdisciplinary approach to the study of language variation in NLP. Her research combines sociolinguistic perspectives with practical NLP applications, exploring both how sociolinguistic insights can inform NLP research and how NLP methods can be adapted to better represent and process variation.
